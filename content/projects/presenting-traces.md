TRACEs stopped being an argument and became a session somewhere in this list — four presentations across just over a year, each a returning conversation rather than a fresh introduction. What changed each time wasn't the framework's six domains; it was how precisely the room could test them.

November 6, 2025 — Courage to Caregivers Summit. The first full conference session built around TRACEs rather than the argument underneath it: walking a room of caregiving professionals through all six domains and how to map a family's trauma footprint against them, rather than just naming that the footprint exists.

February 12, 2026 — FREDLA PPS Provider Learning Community. A return to that session, this time for FREDLA's national Parent Peer Support provider learning community — a room of people doing the same work I came out of, which meant the framework was being tested against practitioners who would notice immediately if a domain didn't hold up.

April 2026 — Family Peers for Hope, Family First Wednesdays. A returning audience of family peer supporters already familiar with TRACEs, which shifted the session from introducing the framework to troubleshooting it against cases people brought with them.

November 11, 2026 — NAMI Ohio's FPS ProNET. The same statewide provider network behind several of the trainings that shaped TRACEs in the first place — less a debut than a return with something to show for it.
