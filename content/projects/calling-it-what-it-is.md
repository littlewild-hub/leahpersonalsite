A formal scholarly commentary — abstract, keywords, forty-plus citations — making the argument that underlies everything else TRACEs touches: that caregiver strain for multi-system-involved youth isn't burnout or stress, it's trauma, and treating it as anything less has produced misdiagnosis, inadequate intervention, and caregivers left to absorb harm that the systems around them refuse to name.

The paper centers child-to-parent violence (CPV) specifically — a form of family violence that inverts the usual power hierarchy and is chronically under-reported, under-measured, and under-believed — and proposes Adverse Caregiver Experiences as a direct parallel to Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs): a shared vocabulary for harm that currently has none. This is the paper TRACEs was built from, and the one that traveled to UConn as a poster before becoming five conference sessions.
