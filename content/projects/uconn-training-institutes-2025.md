A two-hour poster session making the case, plainly, in the title: caregiving for a young person across multiple systems is a trauma exposure, and the field's language should say so instead of routing around it. This was the first formal academic-conference airing of the argument underneath TRACEs, ahead of the fuller sessions that followed at Courage to Caregivers, FREDLA, NAMI Ohio, and Family Peers for Hope.
