Domestic violence services and substance-use services in Ohio are largely built as two separate systems, which is a problem for the people who need both at once and get routed between intake forms instead. This assessment set out to map that gap statewide — where the services actually break, not just where policy assumes they connect — and then to translate the findings into something a program or training team could act on directly, rather than leaving it as a report that describes a problem without changing what anyone does next.
