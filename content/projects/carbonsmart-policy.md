CarbonSmart is a straightforward trade: a $10-per-metric-ton tax on CO2e for industries emitting over 100,000 metric tons a year — power plants, heavy manufacturing, large-scale freight, big agribusiness — with the roughly $100–300 million in projected annual revenue split evenly between two grant programs. Half funds Ohio students in renewable-energy-related majors; half funds small businesses building solar, wind, EV infrastructure, or other sustainability ventures, with priority given to underserved and high-emission regions.

The threshold is deliberately high and the tax deliberately modest at launch, to blunt the standard objection that a carbon tax just pushes business out of state. The proposal is scaled to be a genuine funding mechanism first and a behavioral lever second, with room to tighten later.
