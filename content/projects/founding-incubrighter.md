IncuBrighter is a civic R&D incubator built on a specific premise: that people with direct, lived experience of poverty, trauma, and systemic inequity carry expertise that credentialed training alone doesn't reach — what the organization calls Experts by Personal Impact (EPIs). It channels that expertise into program design, training curricula, and systems advocacy, turning insight that usually stays in a listening session into something an organization can actually build with.

It sits underneath the rest of the work here rather than beside it. The same premise — that lived experience is data, not just color commentary — runs through the Survivor Voices Council, the TRACEs framework, and the policy work in this notebook.
