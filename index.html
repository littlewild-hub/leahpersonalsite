About the work

I build connective tissue.

My work sits where public systems, lived experience, and practical implementation collide.

I am interested in the gap between what institutions say they value and what people can actually reach, use, and trust. That gap is where I tend to work: mapping the system, finding the friction, and building something more honest in its place.

This site will keep changing because the work does. Finished projects live beside active experiments, emerging theories, and tools meant to be picked up by other people.

Public work should leave people with more power than it found them.
