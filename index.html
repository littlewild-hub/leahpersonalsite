About

I build the connective tissue.

My work sits where public systems, lived experience, political imagination, and practical implementation collide.

I am interested in the distance between what institutions say they value and what people can actually reach, use, and trust. That distance is where I tend to work: mapping the system, finding the friction, and building something more honest in its place.

This site will keep changing because the work does. Finished projects live beside active experiments, emerging theories, and tools meant to be picked up by other people.
